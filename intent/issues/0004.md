hv's ruling of 25 Oct 2026, in Gtools (gtools-ic's session): ST0001 WP-11 is tagged as it is, and these two changes to `LogConsole`, which critic-swift's review of Gtools.app's console recommended (Gtools' ST0018 WP-07), wait for a later release, on the record here.

**Observing what runs.** `LogConsole.running` cannot be observed, so Gtools.app's console service keeps a copy of it, for its CMS panel to grey out its own commands while one runs. It also repeats the console's one-at-a-time refusal before it sets the copy, so that a refused second run's cleanup cannot clear the copy mid-run. The copy holds only while the app is the console's one caller of `run`, and while the console refuses a second run rather than queueing it.
- **The ask:** make `LogConsole` `@Observable`, or publish changes to `running`, so that an adopter reads `console.running` and keeps no copy.

**Noting raw output.** `note(command:lines:status:)` takes lines an adopter has split itself. Gtools.app notes a gtools:// click's command, which it ran itself to read its result, and splits its output per stream at each newline byte, bad bytes replaced, to match what the console's `LineAssembler` does with a run. Any adopter that notes a command's output repeats that, and where the two differ the same command shows two ways.
- **The ask:** a `note(command:output:status:)` that takes the raw output through the `LineAssembler`, so that a noted command's lines are split and decoded exactly as a run's are.

**Who it hits:** every adopter of `LogConsole`; today, Gtools.app.

**Size:** S.
